Key themes from open-ended responses

Location & accessibility

Bishan-centric venue limits reach. Multiple respondents urged regional venues and satellite events across North, South, East, West zones.

Youth & school outreach

Repeatedly cited need: engage MOE, hold school tournaments, include CCAs, and attract youth to prevent talent attrition to iChess.

Tournament scheduling

Requests for Sunday events (not just Saturday), longer time controls (30+ min), max 5 games per day, and more ungraded ranking events.

Funding & sponsorship

Donation-only model seen as unsustainable. Calls for IPC status, commercial sponsors, branded team events, and minister/media visibility.
